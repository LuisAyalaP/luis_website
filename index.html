(01)Design thinking

Design thinking

Santander hospitality for the 2021 Formula 1 Gran Premio.

Step 1 / 5

The brief and the insight

Lifestyle as the guiding idea, with the bar as the central piece.

Branding positioned both aesthetically and strategically, and every space used to improve the experience.

Step 2 / 5

The layout

The floor plan, organized into ten zones.

Registration, central bar, stage, phone-charging booths, lounge booths, spa, activities, kids zone, outdoors and restrooms.

The bar sits at the centre; every zone faces it.
